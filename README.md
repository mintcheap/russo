# Azbuka Russo

Flashcards for learning basic Russian from European Portuguese (pt-PT), built as a web app that installs on an iPhone home screen.

**Live app:** https://mintcheap.github.io/russo/

## Contents
- 11 decks, ~300 cards.
  - From class (lessons of 17 and 21 July): letters and sounds (letter groups, Ъ and Ь, полка/полька, unstressed О→А and Е→И), first conversation, international words, numbers 1–12.
  - Themes: Cyrillic alphabet, first words (as a child), travelling in Russia, supermarket, restaurant, meeting people, city transport.
- Flashcards: tap to flip, swipe right = "Sei", left = "Não sei". Direction RU→PT, PT→RU or mixed.
- Spaced repetition (simplified SM-2) with a daily review and a cap on new words per day.
- Recorded Russian audio for every card (normal and slow).
- Quiz: multiple choice, and typing (accents optional; Russian answers accepted in Cyrillic or in the transliteration shown).
- Portuguese-friendly transliteration: "ch" as in *chave* (ш), "j" as in *já* (ж), unstressed о written as "a" and unstressed е as "i", stressed vowel in bold.
- Works offline after the first visit (audio is cached as you hear it).

## Install on iPhone
1. Open https://mintcheap.github.io/russo/ in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share** → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.
3. Make sure the silent switch is off to hear the audio.

Progress is saved on the phone (in the home-screen app's own storage).

## Files
- `app.html` — the whole app (styles, script, vocabulary). Also the source of the Claude artifact version.
- `index.html` — generated by `node build-site.js` (wraps `app.html` in a full page with iPhone icon, manifest and offline support). Don't edit it by hand.
- `sw.js`, `manifest.webmanifest`, `apple-touch-icon.png`, `icon-512.png` — offline cache and home-screen install.
- `audio/` — one MP3 per spoken phrase.

## Editing the words
All vocabulary lives in the `DECKS` array at the top of the `<script>` in `app.html`.
Each line is `russian | português | example (optional) | transliteration override (optional)`.
Put `'` after the stressed vowel (`молоко'`). Card progress is keyed by deck + Russian text, so changing a Russian word resets that one card.

After editing: `node build-site.js`, then commit and push; GitHub Pages updates in about a minute.
Bump `CACHE` in `sw.js` (e.g. `azbuka-v2`) when you change audio files, so phones fetch the new ones.

## Audio
Each phrase has a recorded MP3 in `audio/` (Microsoft neural voice ru-RU-SvetlanaNeural, made with the `edge-tts` Python package). File names are a hash of the spoken text. After adding words:
1. `node build-audio.js > audio-list.tsv` lists every phrase with its file name.
2. Generate the missing MP3s, e.g. `edge-tts --voice ru-RU-SvetlanaNeural --rate=-10% --text "текст" --write-media audio/<name>.mp3`.
Words without a recording fall back to the phone's own Russian voice.
